# Validation Plan

## Objective
Test whether sellers in ONE segment have a painful, recurring order/payment problem and will commit real usage. Compliments do not count.

## Step 0 - Before outreach (do today)
- [ ] Choose segment + country (see `STATE.md`).
- [ ] List 20 sellers you can contact directly (names/handles in `templates/PILOT_TRACKER.md`).
- [ ] Competitor check: list what they use now and its weaknesses.

## Outreach message (edit to match the seller's language)
Hi [name]! I'm researching how sellers manage WhatsApp orders and who still owes them money. Could I ask 5 quick questions about how you handled your last few orders? I'm learning, not selling.

## Interview script (aim for 10-15 minutes, past behavior only)
1. Walk me through your last WhatsApp order, from first message to payment.
2. Where did you write down the customer, the order and what they paid?
3. Right now, how many customers owe you money? How do you know? (Observe: do they check a notebook, scroll chats, guess?)
4. Tell me about the last time you forgot an order, lost a message, or got a balance wrong. What did it cost you?
5. How do you remind someone who owes you? How does that feel?
6. What have you tried before (notebook, Excel, an app, WhatsApp labels)? Why did you stop?
7. If one part of this got fixed, which would matter most?
8. What would stop you from using a small tool for this?
9. Do you pay for any tool or service for your business today? Which, and how much?

Rules: do not pitch; do not ask "would you use this?" as the first question; ask to see their real notes or chats (with permission, no screenshots shared publicly).

## Commitment asks (use at the end of each interview)
Ask for ONE, in order of strength:
- **A:** "Send me a photo of your notebook/chat tracking from last week (hide names if you want)."
- **B:** "Will you enter your real orders for 7 days if I give you a link?" Get a yes plus a start date.
- **C:** A small prepayment or deposit for a customized setup (even a symbolic amount).

A "sounds good" with no ask accepted = **no signal**. Log it as such.

## Sprint (3 days, dates are proposals)
- Day 1 (2026-10-30): contact 20, interview as many as reply.
- Day 2 (2026-10-31 to 11-02): synthesize patterns; show a clickable mockup (Figma, paper, or screenshots), not an app.
- Day 3 (by 2026-11-05): make commitment asks; schedule follow-ups.
- Decision day: **2026-11-06**.

## Gates
**GO** requires ALL of:
- At least 8 interviews completed in one segment.
- The same core problem named unprompted by at least 4 sellers.
- At least 3 sellers accepted ask A or B (strength B preferred) with a start date.
- You identified why existing tools fail them (from question 6).

**REVISE:** Problem is real but a different one (e.g. customer catalog, stock) or needs a narrower segment. Update `STATE.md` and re-run a short sprint (max once).

**STOP / PIVOT:** No consistent problem, satisfactory existing tools, or fewer than 3 commitments by the kill date. Record the lesson in `STATE.md`.

## Pilot metrics (after build)
- Invited 20, interviewed 10+
- 3 commit, 3 record real orders, 2 return on day 3+ and day 7+
- At least 1 serious conversation about paid customization
- Track in `templates/PILOT_TRACKER.md`
