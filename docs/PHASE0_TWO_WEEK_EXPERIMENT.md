# SellFlow — Two-Week Customer Discovery and Prototype Testing Experiment

Experiment period: 23 November – 4 December 2026. Target market: Nigerian traders (Northern Nigerian commercial communities). Categories: Provisions/FMCG, Fashion/textiles, Electronics/mobile accessories, Agricultural/food commodities.

## 1. Competitor check — 5 tools/methods and their weaknesses

1. Exercise books & paper ledgers — manual calculations, hard to organise/search, lost/damaged, no auto stock/balance, profit unclear.
2. WhatsApp / WhatsApp Business — records scattered, orders confused with messages, prices hard to track, balances not recorded, no stock reconciliation, no reports.
3. Microsoft Excel / Google Sheets — manual entry, formula errors, skill barrier, inconsistent files, inconvenient during busy hours.
4. POS terminals & banking apps — no product detail, cash missing, no credit tracking, no profit, hard reconciliation across channels.
5. Accounting/business-management apps (Kippa, Bumpa, QuickBooks, Sage) — learning/setup difficulty, subscription cost, complexity, limited digital skill fit, no integrated supplier-reseller marketplace.

These are research hypotheses, not verified weaknesses of every product.

## 2. Recruitment plan

60 prospects: 20 business owners, 20 suppliers, 20 resellers; 5 per group per category across 4 categories. Markets: Kano, Kaduna, Katsina, Sokoto (plus others). Recruitment ≠ completed interview; track contacted/agreed/completed separately.

## 3. Interview guides

- S-C2 (suppliers): 10 questions + pillar assessment (business management / marketplace / both).
- R-C2 (resellers): 12 questions + pillar assessment.
- R-C3 (reseller marketplace validation): 12 questions.
- Cross-category §4: 12 questions + classification.

## 4. Prototype testing (30 Nov – 4 Dec 2026)

Sessions: business management (30 Nov), supplier management (1 Dec), reseller marketplace (2 Dec), cross-category (3 Dec), final review (4 Dec). Scorecard per participant: task completion (independent / with assistance), confusion points, feedback, most useful feature, improvement requested, willingness to re-test, follow-up date.

## 5. Success metrics

Prospects approached 60; owners/suppliers/resellers recruited 20 each; completed interviews ≥8 (≥3 supplier, ≥3 reseller); 4 categories; prototype participants target 8; 4 sessions; 1 final report.

## 6. Pillar evaluation

Pillar A (business management): sales, expenses, inventory, customer debt, supplier purchases, reporting. Pillar B (marketplace): supplier discovery, publishing, reseller approval, wholesale pricing, offers, MOQ, orders. Combined: whether records connect to marketplace activity.
