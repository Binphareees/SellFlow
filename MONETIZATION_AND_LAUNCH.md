## Positioning
"Know who still owes you, and remind them in one tap."

## First channel
Direct outreach and seller communities you already belong to. Do not rely on launch directories for first users.

## Launch sequence
1. Pilot with 3-5 committed sellers.
2. Watch whether they finish the core workflow unassisted.
3. Ask what they used it for and whether they returned.
4. Fix the biggest blocker.
5. Record a 15-30 second screen demo (fake data only).
6. Share where self-promotion is allowed.
7. Directories only after stability.

## Pricing experiments (hypotheses, not market rates)
- One-time setup/customization: test a small scoped offer. Anchor the amount on what interviewees say they pay today for any tool (question 9), not on a pre-set $30-$100.
- Later paid plan (backup, sync, branded receipts, reports): test only after repeat usage. Price relative to seller income in the chosen market; recurring prices above what sellers already pay for tools are likely too high.
- Never gate basic reliability (export/restore) or imply backup exists when it does not.

## Case study for companies (later)
Problem observed, demo, testers and repeat users, measured time/errors saved (measure, never invent), customization proposal with scope, price and timeline.

## Metrics
Invitations, interviews, commitments (A/B/C), first order, day-3 and day-7 return, requested features, paid conversations. Likes and compliments are not metrics.
