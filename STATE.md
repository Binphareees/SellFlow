## Objective

Find out whether Nigerian businesses of all kinds and sizes have a recurring, costly problem tracking orders, deposits and unpaid balances, and whether they will commit real usage. The sample must be diverse enough to test whether the problem is shared across industries, while staying manageable. The same Phase 0 must also validate both sides of the marketplace: suppliers who publish offers and resellers who apply, promote and order. Do not assume every business category needs every feature; classify each participant's pillar fit as business management, marketplace, or both.
